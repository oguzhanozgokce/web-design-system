## Usage

```tsx
import { Button } from '@/components/ds/button';
import { Card, CardContent, CardFooter, CardHeader } from '@/components/ds/card';

<Card>
  <CardHeader title="Website redesign" description="Due 14 Nov" />
  <CardContent>12 of 18 tasks done</CardContent>
  <CardFooter>
    <Button>Open</Button>
  </CardFooter>
</Card>;
```

The file exports three kinds of card. `Card` is a plain container. `ClickableCard` makes the whole
card one link or one button. `SelectableCard` makes it a checkbox, or a radio inside a
`SelectableCardGroup`. Each has the semantics it needs, instead of one card with an `onClick`.

## Examples

### Variants

`outline` is the default: a raised surface (`surface-raised`, white on the grey page in light mode
and a step lighter than the page in dark mode) with a hairline `outline-subtle` border. It is calm
enough for dense pages. `elevated` swaps the border for a soft shadow and lifts the card off busy
or patterned backgrounds. `soft` is a translucent `neutral-soft` fill that groups content inside
another card or a panel. `transparent` has no fill and no border, for content that only needs the
card's padding and parts. Every card has 20px corners.

<ComponentPreview name="card-variants" align="stretch" />

### Header

`CardHeader` lays out a `title`, a `description`, an optional `leading` slot for an icon or avatar,
and an `action` slot at the end. Anything passed as children goes under the description.

<ComponentPreview name="card-header" />

### Padding

`padding` sets the inner spacing and the gap between parts: `sm` 12px and 8px, `md` 16px and 12px
(the default), `lg` 24px and 16px. The title is 14px semibold up to `md` and 16px at `lg`; the
description is 14px in `on-surface-variant`. `none` removes the spacing, for cards that hold their
own rows, such as a list with dividers.

<ComponentPreview name="card-padding" align="stretch" />

### Media

`CardMedia` with `src` and `alt` renders a lazy-loaded image on a neutral placeholder. It bleeds to
the card edges and takes the card's corner radius when it is the first or last part. `inset` keeps
it inside the padding with concentric corners: the card radius minus the padding, never less than
8px. Pass children instead of `src` for a video, a chart or a
framework image component such as `next/image`. Give `alt` text that describes the image, or an
empty `alt` when the image is decorative, as in the first card here.

<ComponentPreview name="card-media" align="stretch" />

### Nesting

To group content inside a card, nest a `soft` card in an `outline` or `elevated` one. The soft
fill is translucent, so it reads as one step down on any raised surface, in both themes, without
picking a colour by hand. An `outline` card inside the soft one brings back the raised surface for a
single highlighted item. Cards do not track how deep they are nested; the variant alone decides the
look.

<ComponentPreview name="card-nested" />

### Link cards

`ClickableCard` with `href` renders the whole card as one React Aria `Link`. On hover, `outline` and
`elevated` cards lift with a larger shadow, while `soft` and `transparent` cards take a slightly
darker neutral fill. Pressing scales the card to 99%, and keyboard focus draws the 2px focus ring
outside it. It navigates through `DSProvider`'s router. Keep the most
important words at the start of the title, because the card's accessible name is all of its text.

<ComponentPreview name="card-clickable" align="stretch" />

### Button cards and async actions

Without `href`, `ClickableCard` renders a React Aria `Button` and calls `onPress`. When `onPress`
returns a promise, the card is pending until it settles: repeat presses are ignored and a
spinner appears after 300 ms, so fast actions never flash.

<ComponentPreview name="card-async" align="stretch" />

### Selectable cards

Inside a `SelectableCardGroup`, each `SelectableCard` is a radio. The group is one tab stop and
the arrow keys move the selection. The selected card gets a 2px `primary` ring and its indicator at
the top end fills with the solid primary and shows a check, so the state never relies on colour
alone. The indicator is round for radios and has 6px corners for checkboxes, and it lines up with
the first line of the title.

<ComponentPreview name="card-selectable" align="stretch" />

### Multiple selection

`selectionMode="multiple"` turns the group into a checkbox group. `value` and `defaultValue` are
then arrays. `variant` and `padding` on the group apply to every card in it.

<ComponentPreview name="card-selectable-multiple" align="stretch" />

### Standalone selectable card

Outside a group, `SelectableCard` is a single checkbox with `isSelected`, `defaultSelected` and
`onChange(isSelected)`. Use it for add-ons and opt-ins that need more than a one-line label.

<ComponentPreview name="card-selectable-standalone" />

### Validation and disabled

`isInvalid` and `errorMessage` on the group show the error under the cards and turn their borders,
their indicators and their focus ring to the error colour. `isRequired` adds an asterisk to the
label. `isDisabled` works on a single card or the whole group and shows the card at half opacity
with a not-allowed cursor.

<ComponentPreview name="card-states" align="stretch" />

### Onboarding checklist

A short setup list makes the next useful action visible and shows how much is complete. This
interactive prototype composes Card, Checkbox, Disclosure and LinearProgress; it is not a separate
public component. Check the remaining steps, collapse the list, and revisit a completed task.

<ComponentPreview name="card-onboarding" />

The demo uses local state so the interaction is easy to explore. In an application, derive each
step's completion from actual account data and take the person to the relevant task. Do not mark
an operation complete merely because its link was opened. Keep optional onboarding dismissible
and avoid blocking access to the rest of the product.

## API

### Card

| Prop       | Type                                                 | Default     |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| `variant`  | `'outline' \| 'elevated' \| 'soft' \| 'transparent'` | `'outline'` |
| `padding`  | `'none' \| 'sm' \| 'md' \| 'lg'`                     | `'md'`      |
| `children` | `ReactNode`                                          |             |

Every other `div` prop is passed through.

### CardHeader

| Prop          | Type        |
| ------------- | ----------- |
| `title`       | `ReactNode` |
| `description` | `ReactNode` |
| `leading`     | `ReactNode` |
| `action`      | `ReactNode` |
| `children`    | `ReactNode` |

### CardTitle

| Prop    | Type                         | Default |
| ------- | ---------------------------- | ------- |
| `level` | `1 \| 2 \| 3 \| 4 \| 5 \| 6` | `3`     |

Renders `h3` by default. Pick the level that fits the page outline. Inside a button card or a
selectable card it renders a `span`, because headings are not allowed in a `button` or a `label`.

### CardDescription, CardContent, CardFooter

Plain parts that take every `p` or `div` prop. `CardFooter` lays its children out in a wrapping
row aligned to the end.

### CardMedia

| Prop       | Type        | Default |
| ---------- | ----------- | ------- |
| `src`      | `string`    |         |
| `alt`      | `string`    |         |
| `inset`    | `boolean`   | `false` |
| `children` | `ReactNode` |         |

Pass either `src` and `alt`, or `children`. Image props such as `loading`, `sizes` and `srcSet`
are passed through; `loading` defaults to `'lazy'`.

### ClickableCard

| Prop         | Type                                                 | Default     |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| `href`       | `string`                                             |             |
| `onPress`    | `(event: PressEvent) => void \| Promise<unknown>`    |             |
| `isPending`  | `boolean`                                            | automatic   |
| `isDisabled` | `boolean`                                            | `false`     |
| `variant`    | `'outline' \| 'elevated' \| 'soft' \| 'transparent'` | `'outline'` |
| `padding`    | `'none' \| 'sm' \| 'md' \| 'lg'`                     | `'md'`      |
| `labels`     | `Partial<CardLabels>`                                |             |

With `href`, every other [React Aria Link](https://react-aria.adobe.com/Link) prop is passed
through, such as `target` and `routerOptions`. Without it, every other
[React Aria Button](https://react-aria.adobe.com/Button) prop is. `className` takes a string or a
function of the render state, and it is applied last, so it can override the button card's full
width.

### SelectableCard

| Prop              | Type                                                       | Default                 |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| `value`           | `string`                                                   | required in a group     |
| `isSelected`      | `boolean`                                                  |                         |
| `defaultSelected` | `boolean`                                                  | `false`                 |
| `onChange`        | `(isSelected: boolean) => void`                            |                         |
| `isDisabled`      | `boolean`                                                  | `false`                 |
| `errorMessage`    | `ReactNode \| (validation: ValidationResult) => ReactNode` |                         |
| `variant`         | `'outline' \| 'elevated' \| 'soft' \| 'transparent'`       | group's, or `'outline'` |
| `padding`         | `'none' \| 'sm' \| 'md' \| 'lg'`                           | group's, or `'md'`      |

`isSelected`, `defaultSelected`, `onChange` and `errorMessage` apply to a standalone card. In a
group, the group owns the value, and a card without a `value` throws in either selection mode.
Every other React Aria `CheckboxField` prop is passed through.

### SelectableCardGroup

| Prop            | Type                                                       | Default      |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| `selectionMode` | `'single' \| 'multiple'`                                   | `'single'`   |
| `label`         | `ReactNode`                                                |              |
| `description`   | `ReactNode`                                                |              |
| `errorMessage`  | `ReactNode \| (validation: ValidationResult) => ReactNode` |              |
| `orientation`   | `'vertical' \| 'horizontal'`                               | `'vertical'` |
| `value`         | `string \| null`, or `string[]` when multiple              |              |
| `defaultValue`  | `string \| null`, or `string[]` when multiple              |              |
| `onChange`      | `(value: string) => void`, or `(value: string[]) => void`  |              |
| `variant`       | `'outline' \| 'elevated' \| 'soft' \| 'transparent'`       |              |
| `padding`       | `'none' \| 'sm' \| 'md' \| 'lg'`                           |              |
| `isDisabled`    | `boolean`                                                  | `false`      |
| `isInvalid`     | `boolean`                                                  | `false`      |
| `isRequired`    | `boolean`                                                  | `false`      |

`horizontal` lays the cards out in a responsive grid: as many 14rem columns as fit, one column on
a phone. Every other [React Aria RadioGroup](https://react-aria.adobe.com/RadioGroup) or
[CheckboxGroup](https://react-aria.adobe.com/CheckboxGroup) prop is passed through, such as `name`.

### CSS variables

Set on the card root: `--card-padding`, `--card-gap` and `--card-radius` (20px). Use them to line
your own content up with the card, for example a full-bleed divider:
`<Divider className="-mx-(--card-padding)" />`. `outline` and `elevated` cards also set
`--ds-avatar-ring` to their surface, so avatar groups and status dots inside them are cut out in the
card's colour.

### Data attributes

Card root (`data-slot="card"`): `data-variant`, `data-padding`. Parts:
`"card-header"`, `"card-leading"`, `"card-heading"`, `"card-title"`, `"card-description"`,
`"card-action"`, `"card-content"`, `"card-footer"`, `"card-media"` (with `data-inset`).

ClickableCard root (`data-slot="clickable-card"`): the same two plus `data-hovered`,
`data-pressed`, `data-focus-visible`, `data-disabled` and, for buttons, `data-pending`. Parts:
`"card-body"`, `"card-pending"`.

SelectableCard root (`data-slot="selectable-card"`): `data-variant`, `data-padding`,
`data-selected`, `data-disabled`, `data-invalid`. The clickable card
(`data-slot="selectable-card-target"`) also has `data-hovered`, `data-pressed` and
`data-focus-visible`. Parts: `"card-body"`, `"selectable-card-indicator"`, `"error"`.

SelectableCardGroup root (`data-slot="selectable-card-group"`): `data-selection-mode`,
`data-orientation`, `data-disabled`, `data-invalid`, `data-required`. Parts: `"header"`,
`"label"`, `"required"`, `"description"`, `"items"`, `"error"`.

### Labels

Text `ClickableCard` writes on its own. Pass `labels`, or set it app-wide with
`<DSProvider labels={{ card: { pending: '…' } }}>`.

| Key       | Default   | Used for                                               |
| --------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| `pending` | `Loading` | the accessible name of a pending button card's spinner |

## Accessibility

- `Card` has no role. It is a visual group; give it a `CardTitle` at the heading level that fits
  the page, and wrap a list of cards in `ul` and `li` when they are a list.
- `ClickableCard` is a single React Aria `Link` or `Button`, so Enter (and Space for buttons)
  activates it and press events normalise mouse, touch and keyboard. Its accessible name is all of
  its visible text, which keeps it in line with WCAG 2.5.3 (Label in Name). An `aria-label` must
  contain the visible title.
- **No interactive content inside a `ClickableCard` or a `SelectableCard`.** HTML does not allow a
  link, button or input inside an `a`, `button` or `label`. Screen readers flatten the contents of
  a button into its name, so a nested control is unreachable. A nested control also creates two
  overlapping click targets and an extra tab stop that reads as part of the card. When a card
  needs several actions, use a plain `Card` with a `ButtonLink` as its main action and the other
  buttons in `CardFooter`.
- In a button card or a selectable card, every part renders as a `span`, because a `button` and a
  `label` only accept phrasing content.
- `SelectableCard` is a native checkbox or radio input. Its name is the `CardTitle` and its
  description is the `CardDescription`, so a card reads as "Personal, radio button, 2 of 3"
  followed by its details. Without a `CardTitle` or an `aria-label`, the card is named by all of
  its text and the description is not repeated. The check indicator is decorative and hidden from
  assistive technology.
- The selection check fades and scales in over 200 ms and appears instantly when `DSProvider` sets
  `motion` to `reduced` or `disabled`. With `prefers-reduced-motion`, hover and colour changes have
  no transition and the press scale is off.
- The selected ring and the indicator's edge are not the only signal: the check mark carries the
  state, and the focus ring stays outside the card so it never merges with the selected ring.
- Contrast is checked with axe in light, dark and RTL by `card.test.tsx`, including every variant,
  selected, invalid, disabled and nested cards.
